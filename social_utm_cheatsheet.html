Tagging links for social

Add two short tags to any link before you post it, and the traffic shows up correctly in our reporting. Takes about thirty seconds.

03

How to do it

  1. Copy the link you're sharing — the real page on luminatedata.com.
  2. Open ga-dev-tools.google/campaign-url-builder and paste it into website URL.
  3. Fill in campaign source and campaign medium from the boxes above. Add campaign name and content only if you want them.
  4. Scroll down and copy the long URL it builds for you.
  5. Paste that long URL into the shortener, then share the short link.

05

The bits that break it

Do

  • Type everything in lowercase. linkedin, never LinkedIn.
  • Use hyphens instead of spaces in campaign names.
  • Tag the long URL first, then shorten it.
  • Only tag links that point to luminatedata.com.

Don't

  • Don't use capitals. Our reports already show linkedin, Linkedin and LinkedIn Ads as three separate sources, splitting the numbers.
  • Don't put spaces in a campaign name.
  • Don't add tags to a short link — they have to be on the long one.
  • Don't tag a link from one page of our site to another. It erases where the person originally came from.

One thing you'll notice. Once we start tagging, this traffic moves out of the Referral bucket in the reports and into Organic Social. Referral will drop, Organic Social will jump. That's the tagging working, not a change in performance.
